Privacy Policy — HeyFone Last updated: October 7, 2025 This Privacy Policy describes how HeyFone (“the app,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use our Android application. HeyFone helps you find your phone with a voice wake phrase and optional protection features (motion, charger, Bluetooth earbuds, and battery alerts). If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app. Summary HeyFone is designed to process sensitive data on your device. We do not sell your personal data. We do not upload your microphone audio to our servers for wake phrase detection or voice features. Most settings and optional voice data stay on your phone unless your device backup or another system feature copies app data. Information the app uses Information you provide Wake phrases you choose (for example, “HeyFone, where are you?”). App settings, such as schedules, alarm sounds, protection toggles, sensitivity, and battery thresholds. Optional voice enrollment — if you enable voice lock, the app may store a voice profile (mathematical embedding derived from your voice) locally on your device to reduce false triggers. Information collected automatically on your device Microphone audio — only when you turn on listening / Find features that require wake phrase detection, or when you enroll a voice profile. Processing is intended to stay on-device. Motion sensor data — when “Don’t Touch” protection is enabled. Charging state — when “Don’t Unplug” protection is enabled. Bluetooth connection status and paired device names — when earbud-related features are enabled, to detect disconnects for devices you select. Battery level — for optional charging threshold alerts. Notifications and foreground service — to show status and alerts while features run in the background. The app does not require you to create an account. How we use information We use the information above only to: Detect your wake phrase and run Find Mode (sound, vibration, screen flash, optional voice response). Operate protection features you enable. Send local notifications and run background services according to your settings and schedules. Store your preferences between app sessions. We do not use your data for advertising profiles in the app as described here. On-device processing and storage Wake phrase recognition uses an on-device speech model (Vosk). Voice matching for optional voice lock uses data stored on your device. App settings and optional voice profiles are stored using local storage on your phone (for example, Android shared preferences). They are not transmitted to HeyFone-operated servers for the core features described in this policy. Internet use The app may use the internet only for limited purposes such as: Downloading the on-device speech recognition model (from a third-party host such as alphacephei.com) when needed for wake phrase features. Routine listening and wake detection are not intended to stream your voice to the internet. Permissions HeyFone may request Android permissions including microphone, notifications, Bluetooth, vibration, camera flash (for Find Mode), background activity / battery optimization exemption, and related permissions needed for foreground services and schedules. You can deny some permissions, but related features may not work. Data sharing We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your microphone audio or voice profiles with third parties for their marketing. Information may be disclosed only: If required by law or valid legal process; or To protect rights and safety where reasonably necessary. Third-party services: If you download the speech model, that download is handled by your device’s connection to the model host. We do not control that host’s practices; refer to their policies for network-level details. Google Play: Distribution and updates are provided through Google Play, which has its own privacy terms. Data retention and deletion Data stays on your device until you: Change or clear settings in the app, Clear app data in Android system settings, or Uninstall the app. Uninstalling HeyFone generally removes locally stored app data from your device (subject to Android and any device backup behavior). Children’s privacy HeyFone is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help you remove local data by uninstalling or clearing app data. Security We use reasonable measures appropriate to an on-device utility app. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. You are responsible for securing your device (screen lock, OS updates). International users If you use HeyFone outside your home country, you understand that processing occurs on your device under your control. Changes to this policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
