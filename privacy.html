LexCrate Privacy Policy Last updated: 6 October 2026 LexCrate (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is a legal practice management application for lawyers and law offices. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the LexCrate mobile application (the “App”), published by Law Manager. 1. Information we collect Depending on how you use the App, we may process: Account information — If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, email address, and display name through Firebase Authentication. Practice data you enter — Client names and contact details, case information, hearing dates, notes, tasks, fee and payment records, document metadata, and related content you choose to store in the App. Local device data — The App may store data on your device (including encrypted credentials and preferences) so you can work offline or in local-only mode. Cloud sync data — If you enable cloud sync, practice data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and related services) and associated with your account. Support communications — Information you send us if you contact support. The App is designed to record professional fees and receipts in your practice. It does not process bank transfers, card payments, UPI, or other financial transactions on your behalf. 2. How we use information Provide, maintain, and improve the App and cloud sync features. Authenticate you and keep your workspace separate from other users. Back up and restore data you choose to sync to the cloud. Respond to support requests and protect the security of the service. Comply with applicable law. 3. Legal basis (where applicable) We process your information to perform our contract with you (providing the App), based on your instructions when you enter practice data, and where necessary for legitimate interests such as security and service improvement. 4. Sharing of information We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only: With service providers that help us run the App (for example, Google Firebase for authentication, database, and storage), under appropriate safeguards. When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process. To protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the public. 5. Data retention Practice data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Cloud-synced data is retained while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of cloud data by contacting us (see Section 9). 6. Security We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted storage for sensitive credentials on device and industry-standard cloud infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. 7. Your choices and rights You can use local-only mode without Google sign-in where the App offers it. You can edit or delete practice data within the App. You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold about you by contacting us. You may sign out and revoke Google access through your Google account settings. 8. Children The App is intended for legal professionals and is not directed at children under 13. 9. Contact For privacy questions or requests, contact: cpapp2025@gmail.com (replace with your official support email if different). 10. Changes We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy. 11. Third-party services Google Sign-In and Firebase are provided by Google LLC and subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
